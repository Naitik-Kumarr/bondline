Underwrite an agent Judge kit Testnet, unaudited How a cover runs A cover runs until you close it or it pays out. There is no end date. The reserve stays locked until then, so the bond behind your cover can't be spent elsewhere. The price on the board assumes 30 days. A cover held longer takes on more risk for the same premium. Fixed terms are roadmap item 1. When it pays When a cover settles, the bond pays you everything beyond your limit, up to the 30% cap, at the moment of settlement. After that the agent is stopped. The stocks you still hold can keep moving, and the bond doesn't cover that. Usage-based insurance for AI traders, on Robinhood Chain testnet, in Paxos USDG.
