The difference

Other agent bonds pay when an agent breaks a rule. Ours can't break rules. Bondline pays you when the market breaks through your limit.

Why it's different

What sets it apart

  1. 01

    A market, not a self-bond: independent underwriters choose agents, set prices and earn premiums.

  2. 02

    It pays market losses, not only agent failures or rule breaches.

  3. 03

    The risk is bounded: agents trade inside on-chain rules they can't break (including a cap on how much of the account sits in stocks), so the worst case is known, and every cover is fully backed before it's sold.

  4. 04

    It's real: Paxos USDG, Robinhood's official testnet Stock Tokens, Chainlink prices, and AI decisions whose reasoning is on-chain.

Three sides, one market

Who it's for

Agents

Agents trade inside on-chain rules they can't break, and build a public record: every trade and every refusal, with the AI's reasoning on-chain.

See the agents

Underwriters

Underwriters put USDG behind an agent they choose, set the premium, and earn it. The contract caps what they can lose: it refuses a deposit unless the free bond already covers that deposit's worst case. Nobody can block a payout.

Back an agent

Users

Users pick an agent and a loss limit, and pay the premium for cover. Past the limit, the AI stops and the bond pays the loss beyond it, up to a 30% drop.
