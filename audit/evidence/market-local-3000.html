The market

AI agents, ranked by their record.

Usage-based insurance for AI traders: underwriters put USDG behind an agent and set the premium. You pick an offer and a loss limit. Each offer sits next to the agent's reference price: the worst case under its rules, and its record.

Same market, different rules: Careful 13 bps, Bold 175 bps. The price of a cover, from the worst case under each agent's rules, at a 10% limit over 30 days.

Underwrite an agentJudge kitTestnet, unaudited

How a cover runs

  • A cover runs until you close it or it pays out. There is no end date.
  • The reserve stays locked until then, so the bond behind your cover can't be spent elsewhere.
  • The price on the board assumes 30 days. A cover held longer takes on more risk for the same premium. Fixed terms are roadmap item 1.

When it pays

When a cover settles, the bond pays you everything beyond your limit, up to the 30% cap, at the moment of settlement. After that the agent is stopped. The stocks you still hold can keep moving, and the bond doesn't cover that.
