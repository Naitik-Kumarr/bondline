# Audit quality review

Reviewed the report, public contract source, isolated proof source, completed test output and web section without executing tests or making network calls. No original file was edited.

1. **Use the final test count.** `audit/evidence/money-tests.txt` now has **21 tests: 12 passed / 9 deliberately failing safety assertions**, including the new keeper-manufactured-payout and enumeration measurement tests. The working `audit/AUDIT.md:10` still uses the older **19 / 11 / 8** result. The 1,000 fuzz cases assert payout bounds **when** health is settleable; do not imply that all 1,000 cases execute a payout.

2. **The dust finding is supported, with a duration condition.** `testSafety_claimUnaffectedByDisallowedZeroValueStaleDonation` proves an otherwise settleable TSLA account becomes non-settleable after a one-wei, zero-USDG-value AMZN donation, even though its trade mask excludes AMZN. The withdrawal proof separately fails with `StalePrices`. State that the donated listed token must have a stale/invalid feed and the attacker must possess transferable dust. The obstruction lasts until that feed becomes acceptable or the user closes; `testClean_closeStopsAndReleasesEvenWhilePricesStaleAndUSDGPaused` disproves permanent protocol locking. Closing abandons the claim. High for claim obstruction is defensible; do not say funds are stolen or permanently locked. A proposed dust recovery must not simply ignore **all** disallowed holdings, which can have material value.

3. **Replay profit is counterparty-subsidy exposure, not an admission bypass.** The attacker creates a genuine registered cover/account, selects itself as agent, posts an 80 USDG reserve, and contributes **800,000 USDG**; no identity is forged and no cover reservation is stolen. The known low/high historical prices yield **63,428.345477 USDG** fixture profit without attacker keeper authority. That number depends on the fixture's **1,000,000 USDG + 10,000 TSLA** venue inventory. The pinned deployed Replay venue has only **40 USDG + 3.5 TSLA**, so the exact trade cannot execute there. Suggested wording: “Permissionless participants can create valid accounts and trade against subsidized replay inventory using the public future replay path. Profits reduce venue inventory; profitable fills can exhaust its test-token subsidy, subject to available inventory.” Medium demo availability/economic exposure is better calibrated than a claim of unauthorized transfer or a live 63,428 USDG drain. The profitable-trade assertion also fails for ordinary permitted profitable trading; public replay foreknowledge supplies the harmful context.

4. **Both keeper assertions demonstrate disclosed authority.** `testSafety_oldMirrorAnswerCannotBeRetimestampedAsFresh` and `testSafety_keeperCannotManufacturePayoutAgainstUnchangedReferencePrice` explicitly act as the fixed authorized keeper. The latter proves a **198 USDG capped** payout against an unchanged reference; it also asserts payment stays within reserve. Neither proves unauthorized price setting or an absent access check. Group these as the oracle trust boundary / overbroad price-authenticity claim, with Low wording severity unless a conflicting guarantee is established. Monotonic timestamps prevent backward/future timestamps; they do not authenticate source provenance, and equal prices can legitimately appear in new rounds. Exact replacement: “MirrorFeed accepts positive prices and increasing nonfuture timestamps supplied by its fixed keeper. The contract does not authenticate source prices or timestamps. Live mirroring relies on the keeper; replay deliberately gives historical prices fresh timestamps.”

5. **The empty-offer proof establishes scaling, not a known RPC outage.** The **2,000,000-gas** ceiling is an auditor-selected budget, not a verified deployment limit. The measurement test reports **7,888,173 gas at 1,000 offers, with account cold and storage warm**. Creation uses about 307 million fixture gas in total; real spam needs repeated paid transactions. Say “unbounded empty-offer enumeration can exceed fixed consumer/RPC budgets,” and retain the indexed getters as available recovery/pagination paths. Do not claim every provider rejects 1,000 offers, that the site already failed, or that settlements are blocked. Medium requires demonstrated impact on an actual bounded consumer; otherwise Low scalability/griefing risk is more precise.

6. **Pause/freeze findings concern health and observability.** The tests prove `health().settleable` remains true while the mocked issuer makes payout transfer revert, and a paused-USDG trade reverts without a `Blocked` log. They do not prove money moves invisibly or accounting corruption: the passing rollback/retry tests verify bond, reservation, claims and account state stay unchanged and settlement works after restrictions are removed. `Types.sol` does promise that settleable means settlement “would succeed right now,” so Low diagnostic inconsistency is supported. Replace with “The position meets the price and loss conditions for settlement; issuer transfer restrictions can still prevent payment,” or add issuer availability fields/checks. Scope `Blocked` promises to policy rejections; failed external token/venue execution can revert without a retained receipt. An issuer can continue preventing token transfers independently of Bondline.

7. **Calibrate Drip severities to demonstrated scope.** The working report labels the cross-instance race High and absent IP quota Medium, while `secrets.md` recommends **Medium / Low**. The proofs are strong unchanged-handler logic proofs, but funds are test USDG and multi-instance deployment concurrency was modeled rather than observed. State those assumptions and choose one consistent rank; avoid implying a production-dollar loss or observed wallet drain.

8. **The web section's qualifications are sound and should survive integration.** W1 is a **High advisory inventory** result, not established High remote application exploitability: the routes accept no CSS/glob expressions or public WebSocket peers, and the demonstrated PostCSS file read uses the auditor's own CSS and marker. Preserve **7 High entries total / 2 production / 0 Critical**, including that propagated entries are not independent exploit classes. W2 is accurately **Low offline-fixture confinement**: operator-supplied malformed `settleTx` escapes the selected output directory, while ordinary RPC transaction hashes and model text do not control filenames. Do not upgrade it to public API traversal or unrestricted remote file write. W3's header finding is local-development evidence; production proxy headers remain unverified. W4 proves a null-body exception without signing/network activity, not a financial exploit.
